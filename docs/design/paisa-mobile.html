Abhi wali mobile screen se kya badla

  • Wohi Floodlight look: teal hero, glass tiles, dark segmented switch, white cards (radius 24), floating tab bar, bottom sheets.
  • Switch: Ledger / Refunds / History ki jagah Bookings · Payments · Players · To do. Refunds ab To do mein, History ab Payments tab hai.
  • Hero mein "Right now": Aaj / Hafte / Mahine wale tiles (jo filter se match nahi karte the) hata kar 4 asli balance.
  • Filters: 3 lines ke chips ki jagah 2 dropdown (Period, Court). App ke SelectButton aur picker sheets.
  • "Theek karein" har row se hata diya. Booking kholo to actions sheet mein; galti theek karna "More ⋯" mein.
  • Payments din-wise group (web Hisaab jaisa), din ka total upar.
  • Baqi ka ek hi matlab: "Due" = khel chuke, paisa baqi. Dues screen bhi isi number par chalegi.
