ABOUT KHELO

Khelna asaan hona chahiye. Court dhoondna nahi.

Khelo ek court booking marketplace hai jo Karachi se shuru ho raha hai. Humara maqsad hai ke players ko khali slot foran milay aur venue owners ka court kabhi khali na rahe.

MASLA

Calls, messages aur intezar

Slot poochne ke liye kai logon ko message karna parta hai. Owners ke paas bookings kahin copy mein, kahin WhatsApp mein hoti hain, aur khali slots ka pata nahi chalta.

HAL

Ek calendar, ek chat

Players live calendar dekh kar chat se book karte hain. Owners ek dashboard se payment approve karte hain aur khali slots ke liye mashwara lete hain.
