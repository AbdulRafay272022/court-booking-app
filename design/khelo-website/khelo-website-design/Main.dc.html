Kya WhatsApp se bhi booking ho sakti hai?+

Haan. App aur WhatsApp dono mein wahi AI assistant hai, aur dono jagah booking ka process ek jaisa hai.

Payment reject ho jaye to?+

Agar owner payment verify na kare to slot dobara khali ho jata hai aur aapko notification milti hai.

Owner account banao, venue ki details, courts aur pricing bharo. Admin approval ke baad aapka venue live ho jata hai.
