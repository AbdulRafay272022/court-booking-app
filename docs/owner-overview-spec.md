## 2. Overview of days (web owner dashboard home; replaces Today)
2.1 Top bar: buttons **Check-in**, **Add booking** (restored walk-in page), **Bookings history** (section 3). Filters **Date** and **Court**, both persisted in the URL query (refresh/back/share keep them). Venue switcher only if the owner has more than one venue.
2.2 Operating day: courts can run past 2:30 AM; a 1:00 AM booking belongs to the PREVIOUS operating day. Use venue opening hours (incl. closes-next-day), Asia/Karachi; reuse the availability service; document the rule.
2.3 KPI cards: Total slots, Booked slots, Khali (Free) slots, Bookings, Cancelled, Refunds, Pending, Collected, Due. Slots are 30-minute units of the selected operating day and courts. Every count separate: Total = Booked + Pending + Blocked + Free always. Booked slots = confirmed only. Pending slots = hold/awaiting payment/awaiting approval. Blocked = owner blocked. Free = remaining open ("aane wale N, guzre hue N"). Total card shows the four-way split as a thin bar + chips. **Bookings** = distinct bookings (not slots; sub-line "N slots mein"). **Cancelled** = cancelled bookings. **Refunds** = bookings with refund owed or paid (count + amount). **Pending** (a booking count) = held or awaiting approval, with two small counts "approval ke intezar N" and "payment ke intezar N". **Collected** = money actually received (advance + later payments, net of refunds). **Due** = total minus collected for non-cancelled bookings.
Breakdown without mess (13 courts): big number; under it a thin stacked bar where relevant and a one-line "top 2 courts" with **"+N aur"** opening a popover listing every court with its number (bottom sheet on mobile). Never 13 inline lines.
Clicks: **Total slots** opens a popup on the same page showing all slots with status grouped by court, reusing the existing slot calendar component, with legend (Booked, Pending, Blocked, Free, Past). **Pending card** goes to the **Approvals page** with the same date and court filters via URL and a "Sab dikhao" link that clears them so card count and page list match; "payment ke intezar" bookings are not in Approvals, so the card carries a small link "Payment ke intezar (N)" to the History page with that status filter. **Every other card** navigates to the History page (section 3) with the same date + court filters and the matching status filter pre-applied. Popover rows (per court) navigate with that court filter.
2.4 Bookings table below the cards, for the selected date and courts. ONLY Booked, Cancelled, Refund rows (pending/held/expired are NOT in this table). Booked rows may carry chips (checked-in, no-show, Mukammal/Adhoora). Columns in order: 1 Player + time in one cell (avatar circle with initial, bold name, under it time range and duration e.g. "6:00 PM to 7:00 PM, 1 ghanta", muted; plus the short booking code as in the design); 2 Court; 3 Total amount; 4 Advance paid; 5 Due; 6 Total paid; 7 Status chip (Booked, Cancelled, Refund owed/Refunded; on History every real state, with a code-state -> label/chip mapping, reported); 8 Booking completed dropdown (only Booked rows; others a dash). Cancelled and Refund rows show the same Booking ID and a chip "Original booking dekhein" that scrolls to and briefly highlights the original row. Below 768px the table becomes a card list (same data, same click behaviour), no horizontal scroll.
2.5 Booking completed: options **Haan / Nahi** (unset by default, neutral). Enabled only after the slot ended, otherwise disabled with tooltip/text "Slot khatam hone ke baad". **Haan**: due becomes 0, total paid equals total amount, row light GREEN (+ check icon + word "Mukammal"). **Nahi**: popup "Kitna mila?" (amount 0..total, validated) and "Wajah" (textarea with mic button on web); on save total paid = amount received, due = total - received, row light RED (+ warning icon + "Adhoora"). Changing later is allowed and recorded (who, when, previous, new). Permission: owner, or staff with "Record & correct payments". Goes through the existing payment ledger so revenue and Ledger page stay correct, no double counting (backend implements; see contract).
2.6 Row click opens the shared `BookingDetail` popup (bottom sheet on mobile) used by Overview, History, Approvals and Refunds: Booking ID short code `KH-` + first 8 chars of the UUID with a button to copy the full unique ID; player name + phone (permissioned); court; slot start-end; duration; Booked at (created time) + channel (app, WhatsApp, walk-in, owner); hold expiry; advance required; paid so far; due; total; payment screenshot (signed URL, zoomable) + OCR verdict; approval history (who, when, reason); cancellation (who, when, reason) and refund (amount, status, paid-at); completion (marked by, when, amount, reason); a simple timeline of all of it; for Cancelled/Refund rows a link to the original booking.

## 6. AI chat fix (backend, no migration)
On 2026-10-15 31 of 72 AI replies (about 43%) were the fixed fallback "I could not check that just now" (`fallback_unavailable` in `app/services/chat_templates.py`, returned by `_safe_fallback()` in `app/services/ai_chat_service.py`). No model/timeout/quota problem; cause is our own reply checker (`chat_validator.py`) rejecting the model's draft (`unknown_fact` for a price not present in tool results, `bad_proposal`) twice, then the fallback. Mostly on the first auto-sent question when a player taps a slot ("Is <court> at <venue> available on <date> at <time> for <duration>?").
Fix: (1) build the **available / unavailable / price** answers FROM CODE (localized templates from the tool result) and attach the Yes/No booking proposal from code; do not rely on model free text for these facts. (2) Correct the validator rules (`unknown_fact`, `bad_proposal`) to compare against the actual tool results and facts injected for that turn, including price formatting ("PKR 6,000" vs "PKR 6000"). (3) Save the reject reason and the rejected draft in `Message.meta` so logs show why; mask phone numbers in log output. (4) Keep every earlier chat guarantee (one question at a time, sport guard, real facts only, safe fallback last, idempotency, localization). (5) Tests: replay in app and WhatsApp, English and Roman Urdu: first auto question, available, unavailable, price question, "other times", a draft with a differently formatted price. Target fallback rate on the replay set = 0, and the fallback still works when the model truly fails.
