# Device test checklist (audit build 2026-10-28)

Everything below could not be verified without a real phone. Run on a cheap Android (the target) and, where noted, an iPhone.
Build: the preview build from `docs/deploy-steps.md` (Phase 4). Backend: production or the local stack.

| # | Item | What to do | Pass when |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | P1.5 slots don't jump (app) | Open a venue, pick a court + today, scroll so the slot grid is mid-screen, wait 2 minutes (two 30 s refreshes and a slot starting) | The screen does not move up by itself; started slots disappear without the page jumping |
| D2 | P1.5 popup scroll (web, iOS Safari) | On an iPhone open a venue page, tap a day, scroll the slot list to the end and keep scrolling | The page behind the popup does not move; closing the popup returns to the same place |
| D3 | P1.4 booking detail (app) | Open a booked booking: money lines, "Booking ki kahani", WhatsApp / Call buttons | WhatsApp opens the venue chat with the booking code; Call dials |
| D4 | P1.6 photos (app) | Set a profile photo; check home header, profile tab; as owner check Today cards / approvals / booking detail | The photo shows; initials only when there is none |
| D5 | P1.17/18 notifications (app) | Staff marks a no-show and a "kam mila"; check the owner phone and the player phone | Owner push "Bilal ne ... no-show ki"; player push with booking ID, total, paid, due; tapping opens the right screen |
| D6 | P1.19 walk-in (app) | Add a walk-in without phone / without the tick | Save is blocked until both are filled |
| D7 | P1.20 Raast QR | Switch `iban_qr` ON (admin), set a real IBAN on a test venue, open a held booking's pay screen; scan the QR with **Meezan**, **HBL Mobile**, **Easypaisa**, **JazzCash** (do NOT complete the payment) | Each app opens a transfer to the venue's IBAN with the amount filled in. If any app rejects it, write down the error and switch `iban_qr` OFF again |
| D8 | Camera check-in (app) | Owner scans a player's booking QR; player scans the venue QR | Both check in; a second scan of the same QR says it is already checked in |
| D9 | Safe area / tab bar (app) | Scroll to the bottom of Home and My bookings | The last card scrolls fully above the floating tab bar |
| D10 | P3.6 "Pohanch gaya" (app) | Flag `noshow_reply` ON. 30 minutes before a real booking, stand at the venue and press "Pohanch gaya"; on another booking press it from home | At the venue: "Venue par aap ki aamad darj ho gayi" (near); from home: "venue se door"; the location permission prompt appears once; a second press is refused |
| D11 | P3.6 "Main aaya tha" (app + WhatsApp) | Owner marks that booking no-show; player taps "Main aaya tha" in the app (and, with `whatsapp_templates` ON, the WhatsApp button) | Owner gets the notice; Staff ka kaam shows the no-show in RED with "GPS: player venue par tha"; "No-show hatayein" opens the price / received / Cash-Transfer sheet and then makes it completed |
| D12 | P3.4 short payment (app + WhatsApp) | Flag `short_payment_reply` ON. Owner completes a booking with "Nahi" and less money | Player gets "Total X, diye Y, baqi Z" with Sahi hai / Ghalat hai (app card; WhatsApp buttons when templates are ON); a tap gives +5 points; Ghalat hai reaches the owner's Baqi raqam screen |
| D13 | P3.2 balance screenshot (app) | Flag `balance_proof` ON. On a booked booking with money due, "Baqi ka screenshot bhejo" from the gallery | Owner sees a "Baqi raqam (balance) ka screenshot" card; approving lowers the balance; rejecting does NOT cancel the booking |
| D14 | P3.3 points (app) | Flag `player_points` ON. Pay a booking fully by transfer; owner turns on the venue discount | Profile tab shows 50 points + the rule; at 500 points the booking screen offers the discount and the price drops by the venue's amount |
| D15 | P3.5 dues on approval (app) | Flag `player_dues` ON. A player with an old "kam mila" booking makes a new booking | The approval card shows "Purana baqi KH-... : PKR X" with Done and "No-show X / kul bookings Y"; the new booking can still be approved |
| D16 | P2.4 / P3.9 admin in the app | Log in as the admin on the phone | Venue approvals, Owner CNIC, Refunds owed, Dues & points and Alerts all open and load |
| D17 | No-show reversal by transfer (app) | On a no-show booking: "No-show hatayein", type a received amount, choose Transfer, pick the screenshot from the gallery, save; then approve the card in Approvals (`player_points` ON) | Without a screenshot Save stays grey; after saving the booking is completed but the money is NOT added until approval; approving adds it and gives the player 10 points once, with NO message to the player (decision #24) |
| D18 | Dues "Done" (app) | `player_dues` ON. On Baqi raqam (and on an approval card's "Purana baqi"), press Done: once with Cash, once with Transfer + screenshot | Cash: the due disappears, the booking price is unchanged. Transfer: the due stays until the screenshot is approved in Approvals, then disappears |
| D19 | Late short-payment reply (app + WhatsApp) | `short_payment_reply` ON. Let 24 h pass without answering, then answer on day 2, and on another booking on day 5 | Day 2: the reply is taken and gives +5 (after the -20). Day 5: the reply is taken without points; "Ghalat hai" still reaches the owner |
| D20 | Walk-in welcome (WhatsApp) | `whatsapp_templates` ON and the template approved. Add a walk-in with the tick for a phone that has NO Khelo account | The phone receives khelo_walkin_welcome; without the tick it receives nothing |
| D21 | Old app "Haan" on a no-show | With the CURRENT store / old build, press Haan on a no-show booking | The message "No-show hatane ke liye web dashboard istemal karein (naya app update jald)"; the booking stays a no-show |
| D22 | Bank mein nahi mila (app + player phone) | A staff member approves a dues "Done" transfer. Next day the owner opens "Bank se mila lein", presses "Bank mein nahi mila" on it, then "Staff ka kaam" | The row turns red and the day's total drops; on Staff ka kaam the staff member's card is red. "Staff se wasool karein": the player's phone gets nothing. Repeat with "Player se baqi maangein": the player gets the "Sahi hai / Ghalat hai" message saying the transfer was not in the bank |
| D23 | Rejected balance screenshot (player phone) | `short_payment_reply` ON. Reject a no-show-reversal transfer with a reason | The player gets the short-payment message with total / paid / due and the reason, with Sahi hai / Ghalat hai; no separate "payment rejected" message |
| D24 | "Kitna mila" boxes (app) | On Booking adhoora, No-show hatayein and Payment record karein, type a number larger than the remaining due | The label reads "Pehle Rs X mil chuke. Abhi kitna mila? (baqi Rs Y)"; the extra digit is not taken and a red line says "Baqi Rs Y se zyada nahi likh sakte." |
